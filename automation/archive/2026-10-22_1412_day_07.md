# Weekly Challenge: The 25 Horses Puzzle
> **Track:** Weekly Recap & Puzzle | **Day:** Sunday | **Date:** 2026-10-22

**Classic Google & FAANG interview thinking puzzle**

## 📌 Overview
You have 25 horses and a race track with 5 lanes. What is the minimum number of races needed to find the top 3 fastest horses without a stopwatch?

## 💡 Key Highlights
- Round 1 (5 races): Divide 25 horses into 5 groups of 5. Race each group. Order horses within each group: A1 > A2 > A3 > A4 > A5, B1 > B2 ..., etc.
- Round 2 (1 race): Race the 5 winners from Round 1 (A1, B1, C1, D1, E1). Assume order: A1 > B1 > C1 > D1 > E1. A1 is officially the fastest horse overall!
- Round 3 (Final race): Horses eligible for 2nd and 3rd place are only: A2, A3, B1, B2, C1 (5 horses total). Race them once!
- Total Races: 5 + 1 + 1 = 7 races.

## 💻 Code Example
```
# Simulation of Elimination Logic
candidates = {
    'Race 1 Winner group': ['A2', 'A3'],
    'Race 2 Winner group': ['B1', 'B2'],
    'Race 3 Winner group': ['C1']
}
# Total candidates left for 2nd and 3rd place: exactly 5 horses!
```

## ⚡ Complexity & Performance
- **Total Races:** `7 races minimum`
- **Core Skill:** `Logical deduction & pruning impossible candidates`

## 🎯 Interview Tip
> [!TIP]
> In puzzle rounds, never jump to guessing. State your assumptions aloud, divide into batches, and clearly justify why certain candidates can be eliminated.
